Bands of cloud run east and west past each other, as on Jupiter. Every so often a cluster of storms breaks out along a band edge and tears the bands into swirls.

Then the storms die, the jets pull the wreckage out into long flowing rivers, and the bands slowly re-form until the next outbreak.
